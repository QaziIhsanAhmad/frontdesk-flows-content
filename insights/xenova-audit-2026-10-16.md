## Findings
1. **Duplicate Facebook Page**: a second "Xenova Solutions" Page (profile.php?id=61593940532693) with 0 followers, no logo or cover. It splits search results. Recommend the owner unpublishes or deletes it (Page settings → General); I don't delete Pages.
2. **Restricted ad account**: "xenovasolutions1" ad account restricted since 7 Oct ("Pay current balance"). Pages and Instagram show no restrictions. Clearing it needs a payment by the owner.
3. **Net follower loss on Facebook** follows the paused "Shopify Leads" message campaign (22 Sep): ad traffic brought views but not fit; unfollows outnumber follows 46 to 7.
4. **Instagram has no distribution**: same static graphic + long caption cross-posted from Facebook every weekday, no Reels, no Stories, no Highlights, no collaborations; IG reach was 0 last week.
5. **Same post on every channel, same CTA every time**: every post ends "Book a free 30-minute call at xenovasolutions.com/contact (link in bio)" — "link in bio" also appears on Facebook and LinkedIn where links work. Repetition with no reason to comment or share.
6. **Posting time**: posts go out ~13:05 PKT = 08:05 UK / 03:05 US-East, before the UK/US SME audience is active. Better: 17:30–19:30 PKT (12:30–14:30 UK, 08:30–10:30 US-East).
7. **Positioning drift**: IG/FB say "AI & software for UK/US SMEs (chatbots, automation, web/app, SEO)"; LinkedIn tagline says "Turning data into scalable AI products — from prototype to production".
8. **What works**: human/team posts and announcements get 7–10x the impressions of educational graphics on LinkedIn (team reshares). Proof posts (Loinix marketplace case) beat generic tips on Facebook.
9. 1 unanswered Facebook comment waiting in Business Suite.

## Content mix (next 2 weeks, test and compare)
- 2x/week **short video/Reel** (screen-record a real build or demo, 20–40 s, hook in first 2 s, burned-in captions) — FB + IG + LinkedIn native video.
- 1x/week **case/proof** (Loinix, client results with numbers allowed to share).
- 1x/week **team/behind the scenes** (engineer face, what they shipped this week) — ask the team to reshare on LinkedIn.
- 1x/week **question/poll post** that invites a comment (e.g. "Which task would you automate first?").
- Vary CTA: comment keyword, DM, save, follow for series; booking link max once a week.
- Post 17:30–19:30 PKT, Tue–Thu strongest for B2B.
